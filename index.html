About

What I work on

I'm a data scientist on FTSE Russell's equity index data team at the London Stock Exchange Group, working on index methodology, reference-data automation, and multi-vendor data validation across the Russell equity universe. Day to day that means maintaining researched attributes and index factors, rebuilding monthly maintenance processes, and improving how a large equity universe is queried and kept clean — refining how review and maintenance get done to raise quality, widen scope, push automation further, and cut manual review time.

Outside of work I'm broadening my financial knowledge beyond equities toward the other side of finance, building automated research tooling around local LLMs — training and fine-tuning them into credible reviewers. I enjoy working on small, well-maintained pipelines and SQL databases like SQLite, building toward trusted datasets and attributes drawn from APIs, exchanges, and government reporting — along with the steady quality improvements that hold it all together.

Index methodology

index_engine

A configurable equity index construction & reconstitution engine: eligibility screening, market-cap ranking, float-adjusted weighting, and banding/buffer rules, validated by a 17-test suite. A banding study quantifies the turnover/stability tradeoff that index methodology exists to manage.

Widening the reconstitution band 0% → 10% cut one-way turnover from 5.0% to 1.6% in a synthetic study — roughly a two-thirds reduction.
