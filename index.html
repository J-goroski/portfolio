About

What I work on

I'm a data scientist on FTSE Russell's equity index data team at the London Stock Exchange Group, working on index methodology, reference-data automation, and multi-vendor data validation across the Russell equity universe. Day to day that means maintaining researched attributes and index factors, rebuilding monthly maintenance processes, and improving how a large equity universe is queried and kept clean.

Outside of work I build production-grade financial-data systems — a rules-based index construction engine, SEC EDGAR/XBRL ingestion pipelines, and local LLM tooling for filing extraction. I favor offline-first, reproducible, well-tested systems over anything that only runs once.
